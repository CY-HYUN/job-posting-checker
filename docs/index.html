How it decides
  • French is judged per clause, in French and English: "Français courant indispensable" is required, "French is a plus" is not, "French company" is not a language rule at all, and "n'est pas indispensable" negates the requirement.
  • Remote roles must accept someone living in France: a country or region list with France, the EU or EMEA passes; "US only", "must reside in" or a time zone far from Paris does not. A bare "remote" stays unclear rather than passing.
  • Salary is yearly gross euros; monthly pay is multiplied by 12. Bonuses, budgets, relocation and funding rounds are not salary, and another currency is left for you to convert.
  • Experience is the largest minimum outside nice-to-have sections; "5+ years is a plus" is not an ask.
  • Tier C means a hard blocker (French required, not open to France, internship, a closed posting, a required PhD or research record); B means something to check; A means nothing found. It is a draft: read the highlighted sentences before you decide.
The checker reads phrases, so it can miss a sentence it has never seen; the evidence is there so you can check it. Found a line it gets wrong? Open an issue on GitHub with the sentence.
